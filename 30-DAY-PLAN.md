# 30-day sales pilot for landing pages and systems

Use Day 1 as your actual start date. The market order is a starting hypothesis. Shift outreach to the prospect's business days and move the daily send quota within the same week when needed. Keep the search and qualification criteria steady so results are comparable.

## Daily execution

- **10 minutes to discover.** Search one trade and town in Search, Maps or a directory. Capture five unique businesses. Deduplicate by domain and verify the business's own website.
- **25 minutes to qualify.** Walk the estimate path on a phone. Score business fit, specific friction, recent activity and an appropriate business contact from 0–2 each. Prioritize 6+ with one verified observation and a permitted contact route. Avoid submitting live estimate forms without permission.
- **10 minutes to log.** In Notion, record company, city, evidence URL, observation, stage and next step. Log researched and qualified counts, searches and areas covered. Mark a first contact once, after sending.
- **Separate sales block.** Allow 5–10 minutes per tailored message and 15–30 minutes for replies and follow-ups when due. Use accurate identity and subject, clear advertisement disclosure, a valid postal address and an easy opt-out. Follow up once after 4–5 business days when appropriate. Suppress objections immediately. A reply moves the lead to Replied. A signed scope moves it to Signed. A cleared deposit moves it to Client. [FTC commercial email guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)
- **When someone replies.** Ask what service they want more of, who handles inquiries and what information is missing. Send a written scope, USD fee, milestones, acceptance criteria and change terms. Begin after signature, cleared deposit and required inputs.

## Funnel and the US$10,000 stretch

| Track | What the numbers imply |
| --- | --- |
| Activity | **150 researched** at five per day. **90 unique first contacts** assumes 60% qualify and can be reached. A follow-up never counts as a new company. |
| Base at a 5% close assumption | 90 × 5% = **4.5 expected wins**. Four to five projects at US$900 yield US$3,600–4,500 if paid in full, or US$1,800–2,250 in cash if only the 50% deposits clear. |
| Stretch with five paid jobs | Two US$900 pages, two US$2,500 sites and one US$3,200 site-plus-intake package total **US$10,000** if paid in full by Day 30. At 50% upfront, deposits on this mix are US$5,000. Five wins from 90 contacts require a 5.56% close rate and the right mix of scopes. |
| Stretch selling only US$900 pages | 12 fully paid projects yield US$10,800. At a hypothetical 5% close rate, 12 expected wins require about 240 unique contacts. This exceeds the initial 90-contact plan. |

Start with the US$900 offer. Quote a larger scope when the buyer confirms a reason and you have time to deliver it. A sample workload for the stretch mix is 115–155 delivery hours using 15–20 hours per page, 25–35 per site and 35–45 for the combined job, plus research, outreach and calls. Check capacity before signing and schedule any later balances as future cash. Track **proposed**, **signed** and **cleared** amounts separately. The 5% rate is a planning assumption to compare against your own results.

## Keep the work ready for later automation

Keep one record per domain or business, with an evidence URL, check date, market, score, contact source and next action. A future research run can prepare five candidates for review. Keep sending, follow-ups and moving a prospect to Client tied to verified events and the relevant permissions. The pilot starts with manual research and review.
